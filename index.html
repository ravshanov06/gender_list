Ko'plik Shakllari

English Grammar Masterclass • Core Rules & Exceptions

The General Rule & Pronunciation

Ikki va undan ortiq shaxs, predmet, hayvon yoki joylarni ifodalash uchun Plural (Ko'plik) shaklidan foydalanamiz. Asosiy qoida: ot oxiriga -s qo'shimchasi qo'shiladi.

Dog → dogs | Boy → boys

⚠️ Pronunciation Rule (Talaffuz siri)

Ko'plikdagi -s qo'shimchasi har doim ham bir xil o'qilmaydi:

  • Agar ot p, k, f harflari bilan tugasa, talaffuzda [s] tovushini beradi:
    Shops, cliffs, weeks.
  • Boshqa barcha holatlarda esa [z] tovushi kabi o'qiladi.

Sibilant Endings: Adding -es

Agar birlikdagi otlar o, ch, sh, s, ss, x harflari bilan tugasa, ularga ko'plikda -es qo'shimchasi qo'shiladi:

Church → churches | Tomato → tomatoes

⚠️ Exception Rule! (Faqat -s qo'shiladigan -o tovushlari)

Quyidagi so'zlar -o bilan tugasa ham, ularga to'g'ridan-to'g'ri faqat -s qo'shiladi:

Dynamokilokimonophotosopranozoovideokangaroopiano

The "-y" Rule: Consonant vs. Vowel

1. Consonant + Y → -ies

Agar -y harfidan oldin undosh harf kelsa, y tushib qoladi va o'rniga -ies qo'shiladi:

Baby → babies

Lady → ladies

2. Vowel + Y → -s

Agar -y harfidan oldin unli harf kelsa, hech narsa o'zgarmaydi, shunchaki -s qo'shiladi:

Boy → boys

Guy → guys

The "-f / -fe" Rule: Mutation to -ves

Ingliz tilida shunday 12 ta so'z borki, ularning oxiridagi f yoki fe tushib qolib, o'rniga -ves qo'shiladi:

Shelf → Shelves
Calf → Calves
Half → Halves
Knife → Knives
Leaf → Leaves
Loaf → Loaves
Life → Lives
Self → Selves
Sheaf → Sheaves
Thief → Thieves
Wolf → Wolves
Wife → Wives

F / FE: Dual Options & Regulars

🔄 Dual Option Nouns (Ikkala shakli ham to'g'ri)

Quyidagi 4 ta so'zga shunchaki -s qo'shish ham, yoki -ves ga o'zgartirish ham to'g'ri hisoblanadi:

  • Scarf → scarfs / scarves
  • Wharf → wharfs / wharves
  • Dwarf → dwarfs / dwarves
  • Hoof → hoofs / hooves

✅ Regular -f/-fe Words

Yuqoridagi 16 ta so'zdan tashqari barcha f, fe bilan tugaydigan so'zlarga faqatgina -s qo'shiladi:

Cliff → cliffs | Giraffe → giraffes

Irregular Plurals & Unchanged Forms

Mutating Irregulars (Siz o'zgaruvchilar)

Ushbu so'zlar umuman boshqa shaklga o'tadi, ularga -s qo'shilmaydi:

  • Foot → feet | Tooth → teeth
  • Mouse → mice | Child → children
  • Ox → oxen
  • Man → men | Woman → women

Unchanged Forms (O'zgarmaydiganlar)

Birlik va ko'plik shakli bir xil bo'lgan otlar (Kontekstdagi fe'l yoki olmosh orqali aniqlanadi):

Sheepfishdeer

Fishes? Agar "fishes" shakli uchrasa, bu sonini emas, baliklarning turi xilma-xilligini anglatadi.

Always Plural Nouns (Hamma vaqt ko'plikda)

Ushbu so'zlarning birlik shakli yo'q. Ular har doim ko'plik fe'lini talab qiladi:

1. General & Two-Part Items

Police, clothes → Police are coming.

Ikki qismdan iborat kiyim va jihozlar:

Clothes: Pants, trousers, pyjamas, etc.

Tools: Binoculars, scales, scissors, pliers, etc.

2. Context-Free Permanent Plurals

Armsearningsgoodswaresgreenspainsdamagesoutskirtspremisesquarterssurroundingsvaluablessavingsstairs

The "-ics" Conflict & Plural Lookalikes

1. Words ending in "-ics"

Physics, mathematics, athletics, politics, ethics.

Odatda shaxsiy ko'nikma ma'nosida ko'plik olinadi:

His mathematics are weak.

Lekin fan sifatida har doim birlikda keladi:

Mathematics is an exact subject.

2. Plural Lookalikes (Asli birlik)

Ko'rinishi ko'plik bo'lsa ham, asli doim birlik bo'lgan va ortidan birlik fe'lini oladigan so'zlar (Ko'pligi yo'q):

Newsshinglesmumpsricketsbilliardsbowlsdartsdominoes
